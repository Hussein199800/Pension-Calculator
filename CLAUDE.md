# حاسبة المحامي — Lawyer Calculator (repo: Pension-Calculator)

Arabic (RTL) single-page web app with two SEPARATE sections: (1) lawyers' pensions (ordinary / health / heirs) and retirement/death aid per the pension-fund circulars and Law 30/2010; (2) registration / re-registration / transfer fees and annual fees by age (since v3.0.0, 2026-11-24). Each section has its own calculator, report and saved cases. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Same owner and conventions as Sarrafa (https://github.com/Hussein199800/Sarrafa) and Work_1.

## ⚠️ Never lose the user's data
- localStorage keys are fixed since v1: `pc.cases.v1` (saved pension cases), `pc.settings.v1`, `pc.draft.v1`; plus `pc.users.v1`, `pc_session`, `pc_theme`, `pc.lastBackup`. Fees section (v3): `pc.fees.v1` (saved fee calculations, each with `savedTotal`), `pc.feesDraft.v1`, `pc.feeSettings.v1` (`{org, fontSize, footer, signs, data}`; `data` = only the differences from the approved tables, a removed default year = `null`). Pension print settings (v3): `pc.pensionPrint.v1` (`{fontSize, footer, signs}`), separate from `pc.settings.v1`. Never rename or clear them. All the owner's sites share the `hussein199800.github.io` origin: never touch other projects' keys (`audit_*`, `enc_*`, `currentUser`, `activeSection`).
- Backup import is additive (adds/updates by id, newer `savedAt` wins, never deletes). Backup format: `format: "pension-calculator-backup"`, `version: 1`, with optional `fees`, `pensionPrint`, `feeSettings` (v3; older backups without them import as before; each section's settings are imported only after its own confirmation); import also accepts the v1 single-case export `{case: …}`.

## Design — «الزيتون والعاج» (user decision 2026-11-17)
- The user asked for an identity distinct from Sarrafa («الجواهر») and Work_1 («التيتانيوم والنحاس»), "as if another programmer made it", **CSS only** (HTML/JS untouched). Colors were left to Claude.
- Palette: ivory paper background with faint ledger lines on login, ONE olive accent for actions (`--accent` #5a6a1e, `--grad-primary`), dark ink-olive structure (`--panel`, page icons, report headings), saffron highlights and totals, brick for danger. No blue. Light + dark (`[data-theme]`).
- Primitive names kept to avoid breakage: `--emerald-*` = olive, `--plum-*` = ink, `--gold-*` = saffron, `--wine-*` = brick. Use semantic tokens in new CSS.
- Font Readex Pro (via `@import` in `style.css`), Tabler icons. Tighter radii (6/9/12/16), flat cards with a saffron title mark, linear indicator on the active bottom tab.
- «الطبقة الثانية» at the end of `style.css` remaps the old hard-coded «الجواهر» colors and restyles the on-screen/printed report (`.rpt`) with `!important`. The downloaded standalone report HTML uses the embedded CSS in `report.js` (same olive/ink/saffron colors).
- Layout (user decision 2026-11-24): vertical sidebar like Work_1's arrangement but in this site's own colors and code (ink-olive panel, saffron group labels, olive active item); always visible from 1100px, a ☰ drawer below. Account (name, change password, logout) at the sidebar foot.
- Messages via `toast()` / `confirmBox()` / `modal()` — never `alert()` / `confirm()`.
- Telegram backup upload and Firebase sync/Google sign-in are **UI only, disabled ("قريباً")** until the user decides to implement them.

## Domain decisions — union specialist's written answers (2026-11-19)
Override the circulars' examples where they differ; all are covered by `tests/calc.test.js`.
1. Aid 200,000 from 2026-07-01. In aid, part of a year = a full year (9y1m → 10).
2. First partial month: days from the day after death (or start date) to the end of the month, ÷ 30 (death 4/7 → 27 days). The circular's heirs example "25 days" is a typo.
3. Ordinary pension: year fraction ignored (complete years only).
5. Health and heirs (in-service, or retired-health) brackets: the boundary belongs to the LOWER bracket and fractions count only at the boundaries: ≤5y = ¼, >5–10 = ½, >10–15 = ¾, >15 (even by one day) = full. So exactly 15 = ¾ (the circular's "15 → full" example is overridden; setting `bracketBoundary: 'upper'` restores it).
4. Joined after 2014-02-04: at retirement the lawyer is paid HALF of the proportional aid (`retirementAid.full` / `.amount`); the heirs get the second half at death (`receivedHalfRetirementAid`), on the same basis (health retirees: at least 5 parts), at the aid value of the death year. The older flag `receivedRetirementAid` keeps its v1 meaning "received the FULL aid" → no death aid; the two flags are mutually exclusive in the form (if both are set, the explicit half wins).
6. Practice starts at the oath date (`service.start`); training (oath → enrolment as أستاذ, `service.masterDate`, or to the end of practice when `service.stillTrainee`) counts 3 years at most (`trainingMaxYears`, whole years ≥ 0) — the excess is an automatic exclusion. Exclusions are merged (union) so overlapping periods are deducted once. Durations are counted on the real calendar (`diffYMD`: whole months added to the start, remaining days counted; no 30-day normalization), so 31/1/2011–31/1/2026 inclusive = 15 years and 1 day. Net service: an exclusion touching the start or the end of practice is cut at its own date; every other (merged) exclusion is measured in the oath date's own anniversary frame (offset of its start and of its end from the oath date; the difference is its length) and subtracted from the gross duration, borrowing a month at its real length at that anniversary when days go negative. This keeps the oath date's anniversaries (including an oath on 29/2), so excluding a whole year removes exactly one year and the training cap never adds or loses a day. `diffYMD` only steps a month back when the month-added date passes the end (29/2/2020 + 60 months = 28/2/2025). Known limit (user decision 2026-11-19, keep this method): leap-day/month-end conventions conflict, so a rare case can differ by one day (e.g. oath 15/2/2010, exclusion 1/3/2020–28/2/2021 gives 15y 1d); it matters only exactly at a 5/10/15-year boundary. Do not keep re-tuning it for such cases. Brackets compare (years, months, days) directly.
7. One treasury share always whenever at least one heir is eligible (heirs' shares + 1); with no eligible heir there is no heirs' pension to divide, so no shares at all and the report says so; `settingsWith` forces `treasuryShare: true` and the setting is not offered.
8. Heir eligibility is checked at the start of payment and then yearly (annual documents): eligibility for year Y is judged on 31 December of Y−1, so whoever loses it at any date in a year (even 1 January) stops from 1 January of the next year; the share goes to the treasury (no redistribution). Optional per-heir `stopDate` + `stopReason` (`married` / `hasIncome` / `remarried` / `other`): before that date only the flag named by the reason is ignored; other disqualifying flags still apply.
- Join date = registration date (the separate "registration decision date" field was removed; `registrationDecisionDate` = `joinDate`, except that a different value saved by v1 is kept, shown as an editable/clearable field on those cases only, and used for the age-45 aid rule).
- Ordinary pension starts when the retirement decision becomes final: the treasury controller's review date (`reviewDate`) if the appeal right was waived (`appealWaived`), otherwise one month later. A review date before the retirement decision is rejected. Without `reviewDate`, the retirement date (or a legacy `paymentStartDate`, shown as an editable field only on cases that carry it) is used. Health stays from the medical-board date; heirs from the day after death.

## Fees section — owner decisions (2026-11-24), full spec in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-fees-calculator-design.md`
- All amounts in the new currency exactly as in fees_2026.xlsx. Tables 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026 (ages 30–50); annual fee per year 2011–2026.
- Table = the join year's table, else the NEXT one (2011–2019 → 2020, 2025 → 2026), never the previous one.
- Age at join date; any fraction rounds UP (30y 1d → row 31); under 30 → row 30; over 50 → row 50 with a warning (special cases exist), calculation continues.
- New registration total = row items EXCEPT its annual fee + the annual fee of every year from the join year to the end year (active → current year; cancelled / deceased / other → that year, inclusive); per-year «مدفوع» subtracts it. Death aid only for row ≤ 45.
- Joined over 45: reduced annual fee where a table exists (2020: 130, 2021: 300, 2022: 500, 2023–2025: 600, 2026: 4,000); 2011–2019 same as everyone; 2025 = 750 / 600.
- Re-registration: security-struck = annual fees 2011..decision year; administratively struck = 20,000 + 20,000 + 2,500 death aid (age at decision ≤ 45) + 2,500 × gap years + 1,000 prints + 1,000 practice; self-cancel = cancel year + earlier unpaid; re-registration after cancel = 10,000 + 10,000 + same extras; omitted = 3,000 + 7,000 + unpaid annual fees after «last paid year»; transfer = 5,000 + 5,000 (clearance warning); trainee → master = 5,050. ID / license / badge optional (1,000 each). Gap years from the dates, any fraction = a full year, editable. Exempt from training: «قيد الدراسة» (disabled).

## Fees — central + branch and combined operations (owner decisions, 2026-11-25)
- Everything above is CENTRAL (مركزية). Branch fees (فرع) are added on top. Scope per calculation: `both` (default for new ones) / `central` / `branch`; a saved calculation without `scope` = `central`, so its saved total never changes.
- Branch age table «رسوم الانتساب للفروع 2026» (only 2026; any join year uses it by the next-table rule): reg, ambulance, branch annual 3,000, coop, training requirement (paid ONCE at registration: 5,000 ≤ 41, 2,000 from 42), file+robe 3,000, takaful. «مطبوعات + مفكرة» = checkbox + manual amount. Branch annual fee 3,000 only from 2026 (none before).
- Branch fixed fees: أستذة 5,000, transfer 5,000, omitted 5,000, re-registration after cancel 10,000, struck 20,000 (security-struck can be waived per case: `secBranchWaived`). «نفقات إدارية» and «متطلبات تمرين» from «رسوم الفروع» are NOT part of this section (owner decision 2026-11-25, removed): never add them; an old saved `branchExtra` is ignored.
- Any operations together (`kinds` array; old `kind` still read). An item repeated across operations (same fund + label) is counted in EACH operation (paid once as trainee, once as master) with a per-item toggle to drop the repeat (`excludeDup`). The annual fee of a year is counted once.
- Exemption (owner decision 2026-11-25): every selected operation has «معفى من المركزية» / «معفى من الفرع» + a reason (`inp.exempt = {kind: {central, branch, reason}}`). It removes ALL of that party's fees in that operation, including the annual years that operation brings (a year stays payable if another non-exempt operation covers it). Exempt items stay in the report struck through with «معفى», and «الحالة» lists the exemptions and reasons. The older `secBranchWaived` (saved cases) keeps its meaning: only the branch re-registration fee of a security-struck lawyer.
- Operations in scope: new registration, the re-registration types (struck security/administrative, cancelled, omitted) + self-cancel, أستذة (= trainee → master, ONE operation; by the date of moving to the masters' roll `masterDate` (empty = today, with a warning): before 1/3/2026 central 950 as one item (`tmOld`), from 1/3/2026 central 5,050 itemized; branch 5,000 default (undetermined before 1/3/2026), editable per case `brMasterAmount` and in settings), transfer between branches. Out of scope (owner): power-of-attorney shares, partnership/foreign-company contracts, complaints, appeals and other service fees — ignore them.
- Report: «الحالة» (operations, scope, dates), then per operation «لماذا وكيف» + items with الجهة (مركزية/فرع), annual table split central/branch, summary boxes (central, branch, total).

## Fees — learning tables from Excel (owner decision, 2026-11-25)
- «إعدادات الرسوم» → «استيراد الجداول من ملف Excel»: «تنزيل القالب» writes the current tables ready for the next year (sheets: «مركزية YYYY», «فرع YYYY», «الرسم السنوي», «الرسوم الثابتة»); «رفع ملف Excel» reads it (or a similar file like `tests/fixtures/fees_2026.xlsx`), shows a preview (sheets understood, problems, notes, exact changes) and saves ONLY after «اعتماد». Never writes on its own.
- Recognition (`js/fees-learn.js`, pure, Node-testable): a sheet with «العمر» = age table (branch if «فرع/فروع» in its name or title; year from name or first rows; ages 30–50 all required); a sheet with «السنة» = annual fees (columns «حتى 45 / مركزية / رسم سنوي», «فوق 45», «الفرع»); a sheet with «البند»/«المفتاح» + «المبلغ» = fixed fees (template keys or exact labels). Columns by NAME, not position; Arabic digits and separators accepted; «المجموع» only verifies.
- `js/xlsx-io.js`: tiny xlsx reader (ZIP + browser `DecompressionStream('deflate-raw')`, regex XML) and writer (stored ZIP, RTL sheets). No external library, works offline.
- Service fees (complaints, power-of-attorney certification, travel statement, prison visit, foreign company contracts, …) are OUT of scope by the owner's decision: do not add them.
